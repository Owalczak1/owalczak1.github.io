Woodpecker: Why

Small repeated tasks are easy to dismiss, but they interrupt attention and add friction to school, work, and personal projects. A simple automation can give that time back without asking someone to adopt a whole new platform.

Keeping each fix narrow also makes it easier to explain what it does, review it, and remove it if it stops being useful. Woodpecker favors clear ownership and understandable tools over clever automation that hides its behavior.
