Woodpecker: Services

Our menu has three kinds of small solution: a script for a repeatable sequence, a shortcut for a frequently used action, or a mini-tool for a simple transformation such as renaming a batch of files. Each starts with one specific task and ends with a working example.

We do not take on full websites, account recovery, device repair, or tools that collect sensitive information. If a task needs a larger system, we will help outline the next step rather than disguise a big project as a quick fix.

  • Script: repeat a predictable sequence.
  • Shortcut: make a common action easier to reach.
  • Mini-tool: handle one small batch or conversion.
