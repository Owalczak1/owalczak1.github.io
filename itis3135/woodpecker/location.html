Woodpecker: Location

Woodpecker is imagined as a portable table at campus maker events and neighborhood community markets, rather than a permanent storefront. A public, easy-to-find table suits the service: you can bring a laptop, show the task, and decide together whether it is small enough for a quick fix.

For a real business, the exact venue would be posted with each confirmed pop-up, including accessibility details and transit information. This class concept does not reserve or represent any actual campus space.
