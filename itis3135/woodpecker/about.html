Woodpecker: About

Woodpecker is a neighborhood-scale idea for people who have a useful routine but keep doing its most tedious part by hand. We begin with your actual steps, not a pitch for the newest app or a complicated system you did not ask for.

The name comes from the bird that keeps returning to one spot with purpose. Our version pecks away at one repeat task at a time, then stops when the job is done. That makes each project easier to explain, test, and hand back to its owner.
