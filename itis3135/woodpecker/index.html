Woodpecker: Home

Ever catch yourself repeating the same tiny computer task until it feels like your whole afternoon? Woodpecker is a pop-up automation booth built around that one small annoyance. Bring us a repetitive task, and we will help turn it into a shortcut, script, or mini-tool.

We keep the scope intentionally small: one task, one clear solution, and a handoff you can understand. No giant software rollout, no mystery subscription, and no need to change your whole workflow just to save a few clicks.

Woodpecker is a student business concept for this course project, not an operating service.
