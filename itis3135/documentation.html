Field Station Observation Log

The observation log records wildlife sightings along the Woodpecker Field Station trail. Each entry describes one observation, not an entire walk.

Recording An Observation

  1. Write down the date and local time.
  2. Choose the nearest trail marker as the location.
  3. Describe the animal and what it was doing; use a broad identification if uncertain.
  4. Note weather or habitat details that could help explain the sighting.

Reading A Field Entry

Keep the record concise and factual. For example: “Marker 4, 8:10 a.m. - a salamander crossed the damp path after light rain.” Do not guess at details that were not observed.

Protecting The Trail

Observe from a respectful distance, leave plants and animals where they are, and avoid publishing exact locations for sensitive wildlife. Share general trail notes with the field station instead.
